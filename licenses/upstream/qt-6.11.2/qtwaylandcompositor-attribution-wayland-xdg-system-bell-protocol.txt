Source: https://doc.qt.io/qt-6.11/qtwaylandcompositor-attribution-wayland-xdg-system-bell-protocol.html
Qt release: 6.11.2

The XDG-System-Bell protocol provides a mechanism for apps to provide visual notification feedback through the compositor.


Used in the Qt Wayland Compositor, and the Qt Wayland platform plugin.


The sources can be found in qtwayland/src/3rdparty/wayland/protocols/xdg-system-bell/xdg-system-bell-v1.xml.
